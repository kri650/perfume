The Beginning

A Dream Born in
the Perfume Capital

Golden River Perfume was born from a simple yet profound belief: that India's ancient fragrance traditions deserve a place among the world's most prestigious perfume houses.

Founded in 2015 in Kannauj, Uttar Pradesh — a city that has been crafting the world's finest attars for over three millennia — Golden River set out to reimagine traditional Indian perfumery for the modern connoisseur.

Our founder's vision was clear: marry the soul of Eastern perfumery with the sophistication of Western fragrance design, creating scents that honour heritage while embracing innovation.

Distillery Image
The Craft

Ancient Methods,
Modern Mastery

At the heart of every Golden River fragrance lies the deg-bhapka distillation method — a technique perfected by Kannauj's master distillers over centuries. This copper-pot method extracts the purest essence of flowers, woods, and resins.

Our master perfumers then compose the final accords in our modern laboratory, blending these precious Indian ingredients with the finest oils from Grasse, Madagascar, and the Middle East.

The result: fragrances that are at once deeply rooted in tradition and thoroughly contemporary.

Our Journey

Milestones

2015

The Founding

Golden River Perfume was established in Kannauj, with a single distillery and five signature attars.

2017

The Modern Line

Launched the first Eau de Parfum collection — bridging traditional attar with contemporary spray format.

2019

International Debut

Exhibited at Esxence, Milan, and began exporting to 15 countries across the Middle East and Europe.

2021

Flagship Boutique

Opened the first Golden River boutique in New Delhi's Connaught Place — a sensory retail experience.

2024

200+ Fragrances

Crossed 200 unique fragrances in our portfolio, now serving over 25 countries worldwide.
